What this dashboard will not show

No scores without an open method, no tiers, no prices, no strategies. The record layer publishes assessments, never prices; the belts ranking publishes its weights and its coverage before its rows; the ratio pages say what their ratios are not. Anything shaped like "which market should I buy in" is navigation of the public record with a verify-before-relying disclaimer — never a recommendation. The corridor-era dashboards that did carry illustrative scores and strategies are preserved as lineage, behind a banner that says so: the corridor-era master dashboard (figures reconciliation).

Record counts measured 2026-09-11 in this repository's tooling against the live published editions; market-page figures extracted the same day from those editions' own packed records, each carrying the source and as-of date the edition publishes. Values shown anywhere in the record layer are county-roll assessments or public index values, never prices. Nothing here is investment, tax or legal advice.
