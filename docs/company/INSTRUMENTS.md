# Financing instruments, by stage

Choosing the wrong instrument is not a drafting error; it is a structural one that shows
up two years later when a cap table cannot be cleaned up, a grant restriction lands on
venture-funded product work, or a software investor discovers they are in a property deal.

Everything on this page is a **management plan**. Nothing here is an offer, and no figure
is a commitment.

## What fits where

| Stage | Instrument | Why it fits | The caution that actually bites |
|---|---|---|---|
| Friends, family, early angels | Post-money SAFE issued by AGI Corp | Simple and fast; no maturity, no interest; defers valuation | Model dilution carefully; use one standardised form; do not stack uncontrolled caps |
| Pre-seed with sophisticated angels | Post-money SAFE with a valuation cap and a pro-rata side letter | Keeps the simplicity while making future participation clear | Avoid piling MFN, discount, cap and special rights into one instrument |
| Seed round | Preferred stock in AGI Corp or the chosen commercial subsidiary | Market standard; sets governance and liquidation terms explicitly | More legal work, board approvals, charter amendments and a full financing package |
| Customer pilots | Paid pilot or implementation agreement | Non-dilutive validation, and revenue | Custom work that pulls the roadmap sideways; define IP ownership up front |
| Grants, philanthropy, workforce funding | Restricted grant or program contract | Non-dilutive capital for mission-aligned training, access, research and workforce programs | Track restricted funds separately; honour reporting, allowable-cost and IP conditions |
| Robotics equipment | Lease, equipment finance, OEM partnership, or a customer-funded lab | Limits upfront hardware capex | Debt covenants, personal guarantees, maintenance obligations, utilisation risk |
| Individual property acquisition | SPV equity, preferred equity, secured debt or lender financing | Keeps real-estate economics and risk tied to the asset | Requires securities, real-estate, tax, lending and investor-disclosure work |
| Larger commercial rollout | Venture debt or revenue-based financing, later | Preserves equity once recurring revenue is predictable | Repayment and covenants strain a pre-revenue company — the most common way this instrument hurts |

A SAFE is an agreement under which the company promises a future ownership interest on a
triggering event such as a future equity financing or an acquisition. Unlike convertible
notes, SAFEs generally have no maturity date, do not accrue interest, and do not create
creditor rights ([SEC, common startup
securities](https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/capital-raising-building-blocks/common-startup-securities)).
**Recorded as supplied and not verified from this environment**, which has no egress; a
pointer for counsel, not a legal conclusion.

## The planned structure

A **management plan**, not an offer, not a commitment, and not a solicitation. Amounts are
planning ranges; the instrument column is an intention that counsel will confirm or change.

| Programme | Issuer | Planning range | Instrument | Purpose |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| **Locator.X seed — Part A** | **Locator.X, Inc.** | $4.0M | Preferred equity, or post-money SAFE with a valuation cap | The earmarks in [`THE_ASK.md`](THE_ASK.md). AGI Corp is the founder and holds founder equity; this round sells the rest |
| **Portfolio Basket I — Part B** | **Locator.X Portfolio Basket I, LLC** | $6.0M | Membership interests per the Basket operating agreement | Acquisition, renovation, closing costs and reserves for a defined basket ([`PORTFOLIO_BASKET.md`](PORTFOLIO_BASKET.md)) |
| AGI Corp pre-seed | AGI Corp | $1.5M–$2.5M | Post-money SAFE or preferred equity | Core AI and shared IP; the platform foundations of Cognition.X and Robotics.X; central operations |
| Cognition.X strategic pilots | Cognition.X or AGI Corp | $250K–$750K | Paid pilots, implementation agreements, grants | Enterprise learning, AI readiness, cybersecurity, workforce and simulation modules |
| Robotics lab programme | AGI Robotics Lab / Cognition.X | $500K–$1.5M | Grants, equipment leases, customer-sponsored labs, strategic partnerships | Simulation content, hardware access, lab partners, workforce cohorts |
| Individual asset SPVs | Named SPV beneath the Basket | Deal-specific | Property-level equity or debt | A single named asset only — never a general operating-company budget |

**The first two rows are the two-part ask, and their separation is the whole point of the
table.** Part A funds a software company. Part B funds property. Property capital enters a
named vehicle for named assets; it does not fund the operating company, and operating
capital does not buy buildings. A software investor is never placed in an unintended
property fund, a property investor never discovers their capital paid an engineering team,
and grant restrictions never land on venture-funded product work. The firewall is stated
in full in [`THE_ASK.md`](THE_ASK.md).

## What every instrument carries, without exception

Each financing document names the **exact issuer**, the **exact security**, the **approved
budget** the proceeds fund, and any ownership, licence, revenue-sharing or shared-services
relationship with AGI-affiliated entities. The entity register in
[`CAPITAL_STRUCTURE.md`](CAPITAL_STRUCTURE.md) states, for every entity, what an investor
does **not** automatically own — and that column is the one to re-read before signing
anything.

Every instrument on this page requires review by qualified securities, tax, real-estate
and fund counsel before external use ([`FUNDING.md`](FUNDING.md)). This repository does
not issue, market or solicit anything.
