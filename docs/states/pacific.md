# Pacific — AK · CA · HI · OR · WA

State-by-state localization of the [Locator.X process](README.md). Regional threads:
nonjudicial trust-deed foreclosure (Hawaii the judicial exception), escrow closing custom,
tenant-protection and rent-regulation overlays that move underwriting more than statute
elsewhere, and seismic/wildfire/lava hazard layers that belong in the expense stack. The Bay
Area is one of the platform's three anchor markets. Verify everything against the current
source; see the [disclaimer](README.md).

---

## Alaska

**Run the record here.** Boroughs (not counties) assess — Anchorage, Mat-Su, Fairbanks
North Star publish parcel portals; the **state recorder's office** records instruments
statewide from one system (rare and convenient). Large unorganized borough areas are
state-assessed or unassessed.

**Process notes.** Nonjudicial trust-deed foreclosure. Borough tax foreclosure runs to
**borough deed** with post-judgment redemption, then surplus land sales; the state also
auctions land outright. No transfer tax. Title/escrow custom.

**Programs & money.** AHFC (Alaska Housing Finance Corporation — buyer loans, energy
rebates that matter at Arctic utility costs, LIHTC).

**Locator.X angle.** One statewide recording system collapses gate O. Energy cost is the
expense-stack line that breaks lower-48 pro formas here — heat, not taxes, is the survival
number.

## California

*(Anchor market — the platform ships Bay Area editions; see
[`../LOCATOR_X_PLATFORM_GUIDE.md`](../LOCATOR_X_PLATFORM_GUIDE.md).)*

**Run the record here.** 58 county assessors + recorders, essentially all online
(SF/LA/Santa Clara/Alameda deep, with open-data rolls); no single statewide parcel portal,
but the state's Statewide Parcel layer and vendor aggregation cover screening.
**Prop 13** makes the assessed value a history lesson, not a market opinion — factored
base-year value + 2%/yr until change of ownership.

**Process notes.** Nonjudicial trust-deed foreclosure (~4 months minimum through NOD →
NOS → sale); one-action and anti-deficiency rules shape lender remedies. Five-year
delinquency then **county tax-deed auctions** (no redemption after sale — terminal, like
Utah). Documentary transfer tax + city add-ons ("mansion"-style measures in LA, SF tiers).
Escrow closing custom (north/south escrow practices differ); community-property state.
Statewide rent cap (AB 1482) plus stricter local ordinances (SF, LA, Oakland...) — the
regulatory overlay is parcel-specific.

**Programs & money.** CalHFA (buyer programs, shared-appreciation Dream For All rounds),
state LIHTC stacking, welfare exemption for nonprofit affordable; density-bonus law,
SB 9/SB 35/AB 2011 streamlining — entitlement statutes are the state's real development
subsidies; C-PACE active; seismic retrofit mandates (soft-story) are a capex line in older
stock.

**Locator.X angle.** Reassessment-on-sale means the tax line jumps at closing — recompute
at purchase price, always (the platform's Bay editions do). Rent-regulation status is a
per-parcel field, not a market note. Tax-deed sales are terminal but rare in prime
counties; the pre-foreclosure NOD file is the richer pipeline.

## Hawaii

**Run the record here.** Four counties (Honolulu, Maui, Kauai, Hawaii) publish real
property assessment portals; the state **Bureau of Conveyances** records everything
statewide — the only fully centralized recording state — plus a Torrens-style Land Court
registry for registered land.

**Process notes.** Foreclosure is effectively **judicial** (post-2011 reform gutted the
nonjudicial track for owner-occupants) — slow. County **tax deed sales** with a one-year
redemption flavor on some tracks. Conveyance tax tiered by price and owner-occupancy —
steep at the top. Escrow custom. Leasehold residential stock (ground leases) persists —
fee vs leasehold is the first title question, unique at this scale.

**Programs & money.** HHFDC (buyer programs, LIHTC, developable-land programs); county
short-term-rental crackdowns re-priced whole submarkets — check the ordinance vintage
before underwriting STR income anywhere.

**Locator.X angle.** Leasehold term and rent-reset schedules do to value what no mainland
input does — a leasehold with a near reset is a different asset. Bureau of Conveyances
centralization makes gate O one-stop.

## Oregon

**Run the record here.** County assessors publish rolls (ORMAP is the statewide parcel
program — complete coverage); recorders per county, metro ones online. Portland metro adds
its own open data.

**Process notes.** Nonjudicial trust-deed foreclosure (with mediation requirements).
Property-tax system is Measure 5/50 compressed — assessed value grows ≤3%/yr from a 1995
base and does **not** reset on sale (the anti-California) — tax lines are stable and often
far below market-value implication. Three years' delinquency → **county foreclosure**, a
two-year redemption, then county deed (counties sell surplus irregularly). No transfer tax
except Washington County's legacy levy. Escrow custom. Statewide rent cap (SB 608,
~7%+CPI formula, capped) and Portland's relocation/renter rules overlay multifamily.

**Programs & money.** OHCS (bond loans, LIHTC, Oregon's SDC-waiver and middle-housing
statutes — HB 2001 legalized duplex-quadplex statewide in cities); Portland's inclusionary
housing rules bind ≥20-unit new builds (a feasibility gate, literally).

**Locator.X angle.** Measure 50 means the tax line does *not* jump on purchase — the
inverse correction to California; never "correct" an Oregon tax bill upward to market. The
county-foreclosure redemption tail makes Oregon tax-sale supply nearly nonexistent —
distress flows through the trust-deed file instead.

## Washington

**Run the record here.** County assessors publish strong portals (King County's is
excellent, with bulk extracts); recorders online; the state Geospatial Portal aggregates
parcels. Sale prices are disclosed via excise-tax affidavits — good comp data.

**Process notes.** Nonjudicial trust-deed foreclosure (foreclosure-fairness mediation for
owner-occupants). Three years' delinquency → county **tax deed auctions** (terminal, no
post-sale redemption for most cases; minors/incapacity exceptions). **Graduated REET**
(state real-estate excise tax, tiered ~1.1%→3%+local) is a real exit line at the top
tiers. Escrow custom; community-property state. Seattle-area tenant protections layer city
by city; statewide just-cause rules apply.

**Programs & money.** WSHFC (Home Advantage/House Key, LIHTC, bond financing); MFTE
(multifamily property-tax exemption, 8/12/20-year tiers with affordability strings) is
the underwriting swing on new Seattle-area multifamily; C-PACER active statewide.

**Locator.X angle.** REET tiers belong in every exit model above the first threshold; MFTE
status (and its expiry date) changes an asset's NOI class the way abatements do in
Missouri. Excise affidavits give Washington honest comps where its non-disclosure
neighbors (MT, ID-adjacent practice, TX) cannot — raise the evidence grade accordingly.
