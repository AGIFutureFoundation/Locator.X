# Mountain West — AZ · CO · ID · MT · NV · NM · UT · WY

State-by-state localization of the [Locator.X process](README.md). Regional threads:
nonjudicial trust-deed foreclosure nearly everywhere (New Mexico is the judicial exception);
escrow/title closing custom; water rights as a title layer the East never sees; and thin
county record coverage outside the metro counties, which keeps the sample-floor rule busy.
Verify everything against the current source; see the [disclaimer](README.md).

---

## Arizona

**Run the record here.** County assessors publish strong parcel portals (Maricopa and Pima
are among the best in the country, with bulk data); county recorders are online statewide.
ASU/state GIS aggregate parcels.

**Process notes.** Nonjudicial trust-deed foreclosure (~90-day clock); anti-deficiency
protections on qualifying residential shape lender behavior. **February tax lien auctions**
(online in most counties, interest bid down from 16%), three-year redemption before a
judicial foreclosure of the lien delivers deed. Flat $2 transfer fee — effectively no
transfer tax. Escrow/title closing custom.

**Programs & money.** Arizona Department of Housing / Arizona IDA ("Home Plus" DPA
lineage), LIHTC + new state LIHTC; GPLET lease-abatement structures in
Phoenix/Tempe cores (litigated — verify current form).

**Locator.X angle.** Maricopa's bulk data supports full-market screening — one county is
two-thirds of the state's transactions. The lien-to-deed path runs through a lawsuit;
model legal cost, not just the coupon.

## Colorado

**Run the record here.** County assessors publish parcel data statewide (Denver, El Paso,
Arapahoe strong; many rural counties on qPublic); clerks & recorders online. The **public
trustee** system is Colorado-unique — a county officer administers deed-of-trust
foreclosures.

**Process notes.** Nonjudicial foreclosure through the public trustee (with a Rule 120
court order step) — organized, calendar-published. **November tax lien sales** (premium-bid,
9%+ federal-discount-rate interest), **three-year redemption**, then treasurer's deed.
Documentary fee nominal. Title companies close. Gallagher's repeal and biennial
reassessments have made tax-bill volatility a real underwriting line.

**Programs & money.** CHFA (buyer loans, LIHTC + state AHTC); Prop 123 affordable funding
stream; metro-district financing is the standard greenfield structure (mill-levy debt on
the tax bill — read the district's service plan before buying in one).

**Locator.X angle.** Public-trustee calendars are a clean, centralized distress feed per
county. Metro-district mills can double a tax line versus the county base — the A gate must
read the district, not the county average.

## Idaho

**Run the record here.** County assessors publish parcel search/GIS in all the populous
counties (Ada and Canyon are strong); recorders online; state Tax Commission oversees
uniform rolls.

**Process notes.** Nonjudicial trust-deed foreclosure. Three years' delinquency leads to a
**county tax deed** (hearing before the commissioners), then surplus auctions. No transfer
tax. Escrow/title custom. Homeowner's exemption halves owner-occupied assessed value —
recompute on conversion to rental.

**Programs & money.** Idaho Housing and Finance Association (loans it services itself, DPA,
LIHTC); workforce housing fund grants post-2022.

**Locator.X angle.** Treasure Valley (Ada/Canyon) is effectively the market; the exemption
flip on owner-to-rental conversion is the recurring A-gate correction.

## Montana

**Run the record here.** The **state cadastral** (Montana State Library) publishes parcels
and appraisal data statewide from one portal — among the best single-source states; clerks
and recorders per county, urban ones online.

**Process notes.** Nonjudicial trust-deed foreclosure (Small Tract Financing Act) for most
residential-scale loans. Tax delinquency: lien attaches, assignments can be purchased, and
after the redemption period a **tax deed** issues (2019+ reforms centralized process —
verify current form). No transfer tax (a sales-disclosure confidentiality state — prices
are not public record). Title companies close.

**Programs & money.** Montana Housing (buyer loans, LIHTC); 2023 pro-housing zoning
package (ADUs, duplexes in cities) is an entitlement tailwind; resort-town
inclusionary/short-term-rental rules (Whitefish, Bozeman) are local overlays.

**Locator.X angle.** Non-disclosure caps the comps gate exactly as in Texas/Louisiana —
the cadastral's appraisal values plus income data carry the valuation weight, and the
evidence grade must say so.

## Nevada

**Run the record here.** County assessors (Clark and Washoe are excellent, with bulk
sales/roll files) publish parcels and sales; recorders online. Two counties hold nearly all
liquidity.

**Process notes.** Nonjudicial trust-deed foreclosure (mediation program for
owner-occupied); HOA super-priority liens can extinguish first deeds of trust at HOA sales
— a Nevada-famous title trap, litigated for a decade. County treasurers run **tax deed
auctions** after the redemption period (no lien-certificate market). Transfer tax
meaningful (Clark add-on). Escrow/title custom; Nevada is a community-property state.

**Programs & money.** Nevada Housing Division (Home Is Possible DPA, LIHTC); Opportunity
Zones across older Las Vegas corridors; abatements for qualifying projects through the
state economic-development office.

**Locator.X angle.** Check every HOA's lien history before pricing a Vegas condo/townhome
distress buy — the super-priority docket is the T-gate hazard. Clark's roll files make
full-market screening cheap.

## New Mexico

**Run the record here.** County assessors online in Bernalillo/Doña Ana/Santa Fe; smaller
counties thin. State Taxation & Revenue's property-tax division runs the delinquency
process centrally — unusual. RGIS clearinghouse hosts parcels.

**Process notes.** **Judicial foreclosure** (the region's exception), slow dockets.
Delinquent property is deeded through **state-run public auctions** (T&R division sells for
the counties) — a centralized deed channel like Arkansas'. No transfer tax. Escrow/title
custom; community-property state. Land-grant and water-rights title history complicates
rural chains.

**Programs & money.** New Mexico MFA (FirstHome, LIHTC, state housing trust fund); LEDA
capital outlay for job-linked projects; Albuquerque's IRB abatements.

**Locator.X angle.** The state auction list is the single distress feed to watch. Judicial
timelines put New Mexico's pipeline a year behind its neighbors at the same delinquency
vintage.

## Utah

**Run the record here.** County assessors/recorders online (Salt Lake, Utah, Davis, Weber
strong); the AGRC/UGRC statewide parcel layer is complete and free — one of the best.

**Process notes.** Nonjudicial trust-deed foreclosure. After four years' delinquency the
county holds its **May tax sale** — final **tax deeds, no redemption after sale** (bid
methods vary by county: premium or undivided-interest). No transfer tax. Title/escrow
custom. Greatest-snow growth corridor (Wasatch Front) supply is entitlement-bound.

**Programs & money.** Utah Housing Corporation (FirstHome, score-based DPA, LIHTC); Olene
Walker housing loan fund; 2023–24 first-home builder incentives; station-area planning
mandates opened multifamily entitlement near transit.

**Locator.X angle.** No-redemption tax deeds make Utah's May sales genuinely terminal —
title is cleaner day-one than in redemption states, and pricing reflects it. UGRC parcels
let the L gate run statewide free.

## Wyoming

**Run the record here.** County assessors publish parcel search (many via GIS Workshop);
county clerks record; the state has pushed a statewide parcel initiative. Sales prices are
**not disclosed** publicly (non-disclosure state).

**Process notes.** Nonjudicial foreclosure **by advertisement**, with a post-sale
redemption window (3 months, extendable) — the inverse of most nonjudicial states. **July
tax lien sales** (county round-robin, 15%+ interest), four-year redemption before deed
application. No transfer tax; no state income tax. Title companies close.

**Programs & money.** WCDA (buyer loans, DPA, LIHTC — the state's housing finance arm).
Energy-county boom/bust cycles (Campbell, Sweetwater) dominate local demand.

**Locator.X angle.** Post-sale redemption on foreclosures is the timing quirk to model;
non-disclosure caps comps exactly as in Montana. County economies are single-industry —
the M1 jobs-to-rents chain is short and violent here; underwrite the employer, not the
trend line.
